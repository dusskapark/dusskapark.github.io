### I design developer experiences and build the tools behind them

I’m Joo Hyung Park, a product designer working on developer experience at Grab. My passion is **helping creative people create great products that help people live a better life through technology**, and building communities around technology so that people can learn from and help each other to become better creators.

#### Recent work

In Tech Infra, I still own [Grab Console](confidential:grab-console), the portal that brings more than 60 internal platforms into one experience. I handled it from user research through release, and **led its onboarding, creating meaningful impact across the organization**.

More recently, I’ve been leading design on the [internal data platform](confidential:grab-data). It brings the notebook and pipeline work data scientists do on external platforms like Databricks in-house, and I’m designing how people use it with AI agents, from notebooks to the CLI and MCP. I don’t stop at design: I’m one of the heaviest AI token users in the design org, and **one of the designers who has contributed most actively to production through commits and merge requests**.

In my personal projects, I’ve steadily applied open source and AI to design productivity, and lately I’ve been building a range of projects with AI tools like Codex and Claude Code. I share what I build through posts and talks, and I’ve been a [Friends of Figma Seoul](https://youtu.be/rCyZVwUJDOc?t=1840) **organizer for more than four years**.

#### A career in developer experience

I started in 2012 at the [T store Developer Center](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/tstore), working on how developers listed and sold content. In the [ONE store integration](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/onestore) that followed, I helped bring together developer data from Korean Android app stores, coordinate changes in operating policy, and explain those changes to developers. **That is when developer experience became the center of my career.**

At LINE, I designed the [game developer platform](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/gdc). Developers needed to find SDKs and guides, submit game builds, and track their reviews. I brought those steps into the platform so teams could see where their work stood without jumping between tools and documents.

Building developer and operations tools, I kept seeing teams design and implement similar features from scratch. I started defining shared components and usage rules through [LOOKBOOK](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/lookbook). In 2019 I joined the design systems team to work on [LINE Design System Global](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/ldsg), where I led work with designers and engineers across regions to specify component behavior, agree on implementation, and publish documentation. My work grew from individual tools to a global design system and the productivity of whole teams.

During the pandemic I moved to NAVER and worked on integrating [NAVER NOW and NAVER TV](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/naver-now). I designed a product that brought content creators’ experience together and led it through launch.

In 2023, I joined Grab in Singapore **as its first product designer focused on developer experience**. I’m still doing that work in Tech Infra today.

#### Personal projects and open source

Problems I encountered at work often became the starting point for my own experiments. In 2021, I started [Figma ML](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/tensorflow-js) to explore whether computer vision could take some of the manual work out of checking design system rules. I trained a model to recognize UI elements and built tools for Figma and Zeplin that could flag inconsistencies.

I first presented the work at DEVIEW 2021, then at [Google TensorFlow Show & Tell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blL0EQ6wNH8) and [Figma Config 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ckN1QIpjkAk). What began as a practical problem at work let me share an experiment with designers and developers around the world, and I learned how to prepare training data and put a model into a product along the way.

I continued exploring computer vision and computer-use agents through projects like [Figma Autoname](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/figma-autoname) in 2023 and [Klever (Self Explorer)](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/klever) in 2024. Both explore how AI can help people design and build products.

Dongyeon Lee and I presented our AI usability testing experiments with Klever at [INFCON 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JVfpIy5LhJ8).

As AI started to change how teams build products, I spent more time contributing to open source. In 2025, I contributed core automation features to [TalkToFigma](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/talk-to-figma-mcp), an open-source project from Grab.

At workshops, I saw people get stuck on installation and configuration. My colleagues and I built [TalkToFigma Desktop](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/talk-to-figma-mcp) to put startup, settings, and connection status in one place. The Friends of Figma Seoul team built on that open-source app to create [MCP Magic](https://api.metadata.co.kr/blog/mcp-magic-retrospective), which recorded **more than 570,000 MCP tool calls** by June 2026.

In 2026, I used AI coding tools to build and release apps for communities on iOS and Android. The [NaSum shuttle platform](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/how-far-can-a-product-designer-build-with-codex) is the largest: I designed the service and API with Codex and built the passenger and driver apps and the server myself. It went on to win [first place at the OpenAI Build Week event in Seoul](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/shipping-mobile-app-from-phone-codex-remote-seowoo-han-kd9rc/).

#### Community leadership

I share what I learn through writing, talks, and open source. I’ve been part of the organizing team at [Friends of Figma Seoul](https://friends.figma.com/seoul/) since 2022. By October 2026, the chapter had held **21 online, in-person, and hybrid events**. The event dashboards recorded 1,775 registrations and 904 check-ins in total.

I co-organize meetups with fellow chapter leaders and share experiments such as AI usability testing. For the [MCP design automation workshop](https://youtu.be/smE8OMN1Qjs), I recruited participants and led separate sessions at work and with Friends of Figma Seoul. Designers got hands-on practice automating repetitive tasks in Figma.

I design environments that help developers do their best work, and **I build products through personal projects and open source**. Developer experience is the thread that connects the two, from design through development and operations.
