### I design developer experiences and build the tools behind them

I’m Joo Hyung Park, a product designer working on developer experience at Grab. Throughout my career, I’ve designed the environments developers use to build and run products. Along the way, I’ve built tools I needed myself and experimented with new technology.

#### A career in developer experience

I started in 2012 at the [T store Developer Center](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/tstore), where I worked on how developers listed and sold content. The [ONE store integration](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/onestore) made developer experience the focus of my career. I helped bring together developer data from Korean Android app stores, coordinate changes in operating policy, and communicate those changes to developers.

At LINE, I designed the [game developer platform](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/gdc). Developers needed to find SDKs and guides, submit game builds, and track their reviews. I brought those steps into the platform so teams could see where their work stood without jumping between tools and documents.

Building developer and operations tools, I kept seeing teams design and implement similar features from scratch. I started defining shared components and usage rules through [LOOKBOOK](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/lookbook). In 2019 I joined the design systems team to work on [LINE Design System Global](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/ldsg), where I helped designers and engineers across regions specify component behavior, agree on implementation, and publish documentation. My focus grew from individual tools to how entire teams work together.

During the pandemic I joined NAVER and worked on integrating [NAVER NOW and NAVER TV](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/naver-now). I designed a more unified experience for content creators and helped bring it to launch.

In 2023, I joined Grab in Singapore as its first product designer focused on developer experience. In Tech Infra, I owned the end-to-end design of [Grab Console](confidential:grab-console), from user research through release, bringing more than 60 internal platforms into one consistent experience. I led the console's successful onboarding, creating meaningful impact across the organization.

In 2026, I expanded my work to an [internal data platform](confidential:grab-data), bringing data scientists’ notebook and pipeline environments in-house. The aim was to build internal capabilities like those offered by external data analytics and machine learning platforms such as Databricks. As lead designer and product builder, I designed end-to-end AI-agentic workflows across Python notebooks, IDE plugins, CLI and MCP tools, and pipeline management, and contributed many code commits to the product.

#### Personal projects and open source

Problems I encountered at work often became the starting point for my own experiments. In 2021, I started [Figma ML](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/tensorflow-js) to explore whether computer vision could take some of the manual work out of checking design system rules. I trained a model to recognize UI elements and built tools for Figma and Zeplin that could flag inconsistencies.

I shared the work at DEVIEW 2021, [Google TensorFlow Show & Tell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=blL0EQ6wNH8), and [Figma Config 2022](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ckN1QIpjkAk). What began as a practical maintenance problem gave me a chance to learn how to prepare training data, put a model into a product, and share the results with designers and developers around the world.

I continued exploring computer vision and computer-use agents through projects like [Figma Autoname](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/figma-autoname) in 2023 and [Klever (Self Explorer)](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/klever) in 2024. Both explore how AI can help people design and build products.

Dongyeon Lee and I presented our AI usability testing experiments with Klever at Inflearn’s [INFCON 2024](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JVfpIy5LhJ8).

As AI started to change how teams build products, I spent more time contributing to open source. In 2025, I contributed core automation features to [TalkToFigma](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/talk-to-figma-mcp), an open-source project from Grab.

At workshops, I saw people get stuck on installation and configuration. My colleagues and I built [TalkToFigma Desktop](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/talk-to-figma-mcp) to put startup, settings, and connection status in one place. The Friends of Figma Seoul team built on that open-source app to create [MCP Magic](https://api.metadata.co.kr/blog/mcp-magic-retrospective), which recorded more than 570,000 MCP tool calls by June 2026.

In 2026, I used AI coding tools to build and release apps for communities on iOS and Android. For the [NaSum shuttle platform](https://api.metadata.co.kr/project/how-far-can-a-product-designer-build-with-codex), I designed the service and API with Codex and built the passenger and driver apps and their server myself. The project won [first place at OpenAI Builder Day Seoul](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/shipping-mobile-app-from-phone-codex-remote-seowoo-han-kd9rc/).

#### Community leadership

I share what I learn through writing, talks, and open source. I’ve been part of the organizing team at [Friends of Figma Seoul](https://friends.figma.com/seoul/) since around 2020. By October 2026, the chapter had held 21 online, in-person, and hybrid events. The event dashboards recorded 1,775 registrations and 904 check-ins in total.

I co-organize meetups with fellow chapter leaders and share experiments such as AI usability testing. For the [MCP design automation workshop](https://youtu.be/smE8OMN1Qjs), I recruited participants and led separate sessions at work and with Friends of Figma Seoul. Designers got hands-on practice automating repetitive tasks in Figma.

I design environments that help developers do their best work, and I build products through personal projects and open source. Developer experience is the thread that connects the two, from design through development and operations.
